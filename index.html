FAQ

Questions Answered

Everything you need to know to plan the perfect celebration with LittleLux.

Still have questions? →

We recommend booking at least 4–6 weeks in advance, especially for weekend dates. Our most popular themes (Princess, Superhero) and the Grand Ballroom are in high demand. For peak seasons (May–July), 8–10 weeks is ideal.

External cakes are welcome with prior approval. For decorations, our packages include all décor — adding external items may incur a small setup fee. We encourage using our in-house bakers who create extraordinary themed cakes.

LittleLux is designed for children aged 1–12, with different packages tailored for toddlers, young children, and pre-teens. Our event team customizes activities, entertainment, and food menus based on the age group.

Cancellations 30+ days before the event receive a full refund. 15–29 days: 50% refund. Within 14 days: non-refundable, but we offer a one-time reschedule option within 6 months.

Yes! We offer complimentary parking for 50+ vehicles. Valet parking service is available for Premium & Royale packages. For larger events, additional parking coordination can be arranged.
