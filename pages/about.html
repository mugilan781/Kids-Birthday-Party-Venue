Our Journey

Born from a Mother's Dream

LittleLux began in 2018 when Preethi Arora, a mother of two, realized that Hyderabad lacked a truly luxury, magical birthday venue designed specifically for children. She wanted a space where every child felt like royalty and every parent could exhale with complete confidence.

Starting with a single hall and a passionate team of five, LittleLux quickly became the city's most-talked-about celebration destination. Today, we operate three magnificent halls and have crafted over 2,400 unforgettable birthday stories.

Every ribbon tied, every candle lit, every balloon arranged is an act of love — because we believe every child deserves to feel extraordinary on their special day.
