Our Journey

Born from a Mother's Dream

LittleLux began in 2018 when Preethi Arora, a mother of two, realized that Hyderabad lacked a truly luxury, magical birthday venue designed specifically for children. She wanted a space where every child felt like royalty and every parent could exhale with complete confidence.

Starting with a single hall and a passionate team of five, LittleLux quickly became the city's most-talked-about celebration destination. Today, we operate three magnificent halls and have crafted over 2,400 unforgettable birthday stories.

Every ribbon tied, every candle lit, every balloon arranged is an act of love — because we believe every child deserves to feel extraordinary on their special day.

What We Believe

Our Core Values

Magical Excellence

We refuse to settle for ordinary. Every element of your celebration is curated to exceed expectations and create genuine wonder.

Child-First Approach

Every decision we make starts with one question: will this make the birthday child feel truly special? Our venues, activities and food are all designed with children's delight in mind.

Parent Partnership

We know entrusting your child's birthday to someone else takes trust. We earn it through transparent communication, professional execution and genuine care.

Sustainable Luxury

We source organic foods, use eco-friendly decorations and offset our carbon footprint — because the most magical gift to children is a beautiful planet.

Creative Innovation

Our design team constantly evolves our themes, activities and experiences to remain at the cutting edge of luxury children's event design.

Safety & Trust

CCTV coverage, child-safe materials, certified catering and fully trained staff ensure every celebration is as safe as it is spectacular.
