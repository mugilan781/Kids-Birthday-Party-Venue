Effective Date: September 1, 2026. LittleLux ("we," "our," "us") is dedicated to upholding the highest standards of privacy and data security for parents, guardians, and guests who entrust us with their celebration details.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide Directly

  • Parent / Guardian Details: Full name, verified email address, mobile phone number, and residential billing address.
  • Event Planning Details: Child's first name, age turning, preferred celebration theme, guest count, dietary preferences, and custom notes.
  • Payment Transactions: Advance and balance payment records (all processed securely via PCI-DSS compliant gateways like Razorpay and Stripe — we never store full credit/debit card credentials on our servers).
  • Inquiries & Messages: Questions submitted through our contact forms or booking consultation requests.

1.2 Automatically Collected Information

  • Device type, browser version, IP address, and general geographic location.
  • Page interactions, session durations, and referral sources to optimize website performance.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To schedule, confirm, and coordinate your child's luxury party experience.
  • To transmit real-time booking alerts, balance reminders, and day-of event itineraries.
  • To personalize theme décor, dietary catering menus, and bespoke cake presentations.
  • To maintain secure parent dashboard accounts with historical booking records.
  • To solicit post-event feedback and improve our guest hospitality experience.

3. Information Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Information is shared strictly on a need-to-know basis with:

  • Dedicated Event Specialists: Executive chefs, theme decorators, and certified entertainers working directly on your confirmed booking.
  • Secure Payment Gateways: Authorized banking and payment gateways for instant transaction processing.
  • Regulatory Authorities: When required by statutory Indian laws or legal compliance mandates.

4. Cookies & Tracking

We use essential cookies to maintain your active login session, preserve your visual preferences (Dark Mode / Light Mode and RTL orientation), and analyze aggregated web traffic. You can adjust cookie preferences in your browser at any time.

5. Children's Privacy

🔒 Parent-Controlled Access: While LittleLux creates magical experiences for children, our website, registration portal, and dashboard are intended strictly for adults aged 18 and older. We do not solicit personal contact information directly from minors.

6. Data Security

We utilize industry-grade 256-bit SSL encryption, automated firewall defenses, strict role-based access protocols, and regular penetration audits to safeguard your account information.

7. Your Privacy Rights

You have full autonomy over your data. At any time, you may request:

  • Access to a complete digital copy of your personal data.
  • Correction of any out-of-date or inaccurate profile information.
  • Complete erasure of your account and non-statutory booking records.
  • Opt-out from marketing circulars or event inspiration newsletters.

8. Data Retention

We retain transaction and booking records for up to 7 years in accordance with Indian tax and corporate regulatory requirements. Non-essential consultation inquiries are archived or purged after 12 months.
