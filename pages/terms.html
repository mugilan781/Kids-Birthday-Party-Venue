Last Updated: September 1, 2026. These Terms and Conditions constitute a binding agreement between LittleLux Events Pvt. Ltd. and the reserving client. By confirming a booking or remitting an advance deposit, you acknowledge and accept these terms.

1. Definitions

  • "LittleLux" / "Venue": Refers to LittleLux Events Pvt. Ltd. and its dedicated premises at 12 Celebration Avenue, Jubilee Hills, Hyderabad.
  • "Client": The adult individual (parent, guardian, or representative) contracting the event.
  • "Event": The scheduled kids celebration, theme party, or milestone gathering.
  • "Package": The chosen tier (Sparkle Soirée, Magic Moments, or Grand Royale).

2. Booking & Confirmation

A booking slot is officially reserved and locked upon receipt of the mandatory 50% initial advance deposit and issuance of an official LittleLux Digital Booking Voucher. Unconfirmed slot inquiries are held tentatively for a maximum of 48 hours before being released to waiting clients.

  • All event reservations are subject to venue availability and ballroom capacity limits.
  • Accurate guest counts (adults and children) must be provided no later than 14 business days prior to the event.
  • LittleLux reserves the right to decline any booking that conflicts with venue safety codes or values.

3. Payment Milestones

  • Advance Deposit: 50% of the base package price is required at the time of reservation.
  • Final Balance: The remaining 50% balance plus confirmed add-ons must be settled 7 calendar days before the event date.
  • Accepted Channels: Net banking (NEFT/RTGS), UPI, debit/credit cards, and verified corporate cheques.
  • Taxes: All advertised prices are exclusive of statutory GST (18%), which will be itemized on the final tax invoice.

4. Cancellation & Rescheduling

Fair Notice Refund Schedule: We understand that life with young children can be unpredictable. Our tiered refund policy is designed to be as supportive and fair as possible.

  • 30+ Days Prior: 75% refund of advance deposit (25% administrative fee retained).
  • 15 to 29 Days Prior: 50% refund of advance deposit.
  • 7 to 14 Days Prior: Advance deposit is non-refundable; eligible for complimentary date rescheduling within 90 days.
  • Under 7 Days: Non-refundable due to fresh chef procurement and custom theme construction already underway.
  • Emergency Rescheduling: In certified medical situations, our management will issue a 100% credit toward any future date within 6 months.

5. Venue Rules & Etiquette

  • Outside Food: In accordance with HACCP food hygiene guidelines, outside catering or unverified food items are strictly prohibited (licensed designer specialty cakes excepted with prior notice).
  • Safety & Décor: Open flames, pyrotechnics, smoke flares, and metallic glitter bombs are prohibited. Professional electric sparkler fountains provided by LittleLux staff are allowed.
  • Supervision: An appropriate adult-to-child ratio is required. LittleLux provides trained play monitors, but parental oversight remains essential.
  • Event Duration: The celebration begins and concludes strictly per the booked session time. An optional 30-minute buffer is provided for guest departures.

6. Liability & Safety

LittleLux maintains state-of-the-art soft-padded play zones, child-safe furniture, air purification, and full public liability insurance coverage. Clients are responsible for informing our culinary staff of severe allergies (nuts, dairy, gluten) during menu finalization.

7. Media & Photography

All high-resolution photographs and cinematic 4K highlight reels produced under your package will be delivered via a private, password-protected digital gallery within 10 days post-event. LittleLux may feature select aesthetic venue photos in portfolio showcases unless the client opts out in writing prior to the celebration.

8. Dispute Resolution

Any dispute arising under these Terms shall be resolved amicably through mutual executive consultation. If unresolved within 30 days, the matter shall fall under the exclusive jurisdiction of the civil courts of Hyderabad, Telangana, India.

9. Policy Modifications

LittleLux reserves the right to amend venue policies in accordance with industry best practices and municipal regulations. Confirmed bookings will adhere to the terms active on their date of reservation.
