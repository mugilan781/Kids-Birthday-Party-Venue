Venue Guidelines

Venue Rules & Policies

A few simple guidelines to ensure every celebration runs safely and beautifully.

⏰
Timing
Events must conclude within the booked duration. 15-minute overtime grace allowed; ₹2,000 per additional 30 minutes thereafter.
🚫
Prohibited Items
No external alcohol, fireworks, confetti cannons, or glitter products. Only LED candles on cakes within the venue.
👶
Child Safety
All play equipment is ASTM-certified. Adult supervision required at all activity zones. Our trained staff provide support throughout.
🐾
Pets Policy
We love animals, but for guest safety and hygiene, pets are not permitted inside the venue premises. Support animals are an exception.
🍕
External Food
Our kitchen handles all catering. External food is not permitted except for dietary-specific items approved in advance by our team.
💰
Payment Policy
50% advance to confirm booking. Remaining balance 7 days before event. We accept UPI, card, bank transfer and EMI options.
